Benta Q, See U! is a dedicated online buy-and-sell platform built exclusively for the Quezon City University community.

FAQs

Who can use the website?

QCU students are the primary users of the platform.

Can I pay directly through the website?

No, payments are arranged between buyers and sellers.

How do I contact a seller?

You can communicate with sellers through product inquiries.
